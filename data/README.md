# Demo data

All records in this directory are **synthetic and fictional**. They do not represent real issuers, market prices, news reports, or investment recommendations.

- `synthetic_financial_events.jsonl`: fictional event text from financial-news and social-media source categories for ingestion/analysis demonstrations.
- `synthetic_portfolio.csv`: fictional holdings and illustrative USD valuations for portfolio and stress-test demonstrations.

Prices and scenario shocks are assumptions for software demonstration only. No confidential or client data is included.
